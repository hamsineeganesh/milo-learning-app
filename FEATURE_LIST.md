# MILO Project Features

MILO is a story-led learning web application designed with neurodivergent children and their caregivers in mind. The prototype brings interactive child activities and a caregiver dashboard together. The aims below describe the skills each activity is designed to practise; they are not guarantees of a learning outcome and do not assess or diagnose a child.

## 1. Home and role selection

The home screen provides separate tiles for caregivers and children.

## 2. Child profile

Caregivers can create a profile with the child's name and age. Profile information is saved in the current browser for this prototype.

**Aim:** Give the app a simple way to display the child's profile in the caregiver dashboard and personalise the local experience.

## 3. Adventure character choice

The child can choose an illustrated adventure companion, such as Chutki, Mia, Levi, or Ash. The selected character appears in the story and garden map.

**Aim:** Offer a child a familiar choice and a sense of participation before beginning an activity.

## 4. Garden map and level progress

The map presents the learning activities as levels. Progress and completed-level information are saved in browser storage, and completing an activity can update the map or unlock the next level.

**Aim:** Help the child understand where they are in the adventure and help the caregiver see which activities have been completed.

## 5. Level 1 - The Little Seed

The child follows an illustrated story about planting and caring for a seed, answers story questions, and then completes an interactive planting activity. The story and activity guide the child through steps such as preparing soil, planting, watering, and caring for a growing plant.

**Learning aims:**

- Practise following a short sequence of steps.
- Explore basic ideas about what a plant needs to grow.
- Practise making choices and connecting actions with what happens next.
- Encourage care, patience, and responsibility through the story theme.
- Provide opportunities to listen, read, respond, and use gentle feedback.

## 6. Level 2 - Smart Shopping and Saving

The child enters a shopping activity with an INR 100 budget. They can choose from a set of items, add choices to a trolley, see the amount remaining, and complete a follow-on coin-saving activity by moving coins from a purse to a piggy bank.

**Learning aims:**

- Practise recognising prices and thinking about amounts of money.
- Explore choosing within a budget and noticing how purchases affect the money left.
- Practise comparing options and making a simple plan.
- Introduce the idea of saving some money for later through the piggy-bank activity.
- Support turn-taking with activity steps, attention to feedback, and everyday decision-making.

The activity is a learning game, not financial advice. Choices can be discussed or adapted by the caregiver.

## 7. Caregiver dashboard

The caregiver area provides the child's profile and progress information. Dashboard sections include progress and growth, skills or learning themes, learning history, recommendations, and settings where available.

**Aim:** Make activity completion easier for caregivers to review and provide a place to reflect on the child's experience.

## 8. Caregiver preferences

Caregivers can adjust the preferences available in the app, such as sound, narration, or visual comfort, to better suit the child's sensory preferences.

**Aim:** Let caregivers choose options that make the session more comfortable for their child.

Preferences are caregiver controls; they do not mean the app automatically detects sensory needs.

## 9. Ask MILO - AI Parent Guide

Ask MILO is a caregiver-facing interface where a caregiver can describe an everyday learning concern and optionally provide a learning goal, the child's interests, and a preferred activity style. The intended Gemini-generated guide is organised into sections such as understanding the child's perspective, practical strategies, a shared activity, gentle phrases, adaptations, signs the child may need a break, and a reflection question.

**Aim:** Offer caregivers practical ideas they can consider and adapt, using the context they choose to share.

**Current status:** Ask MILO works when the project is configured and run locally. Its Render deployment is still being resolved.

**Safety:** Ask MILO is educational support, not diagnosis or medical care. It must not recommend changing prescribed treatment or replacing professional support. Caregivers should avoid entering identifying or sensitive information about a child.
